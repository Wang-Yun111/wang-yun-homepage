# Multimodal Perception for Rocket Recovery

A research and engineering project on **multi-sensor online perception for end-stage rocket recovery**.

## System

The pipeline contains synchronized data management, RGB localization, LiDAR localization, result-level fusion and state estimation.

## What I worked on

- RGB and LiDAR localization pipelines
- Multi-source synchronization and common output contracts
- Confidence-aware result fusion
- Dynamic state estimation and closed-loop validation
- Sensor layout, coverage analysis and engineering packaging

## Why this project matters to me

Different sensors observe the same physical state through very different measurement processes. Robust perception therefore depends on both geometry and representation design.

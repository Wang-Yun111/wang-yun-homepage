# Understanding 3D Gaussian Splatting

## The object being represented

A 3D Gaussian can be described by a center, covariance, opacity and appearance attributes. The covariance determines the local spatial extent and orientation of the primitive.

## Why covariance matters

Rather than treating every point as infinitesimal, the Gaussian gives each primitive an anisotropic support region. This makes the representation both continuous and differentiable.

I am interested in whether Gaussian representations can be adapted to scientific 3D fields where observations are dense in some dimensions and extremely sparse in others.

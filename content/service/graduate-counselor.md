# Graduate Student Counselor

Alongside research, I serve as a **part-time graduate student counselor** at Beihang University.

My work focuses on helping incoming graduate students adapt to university life, coordinating communication between students and the school, and supporting community activities throughout the academic year.

## What I do

- New-student orientation and transition support
- Student communication and information coordination
- Class and community organization
- Student activities and event support
- Ongoing mentoring and day-to-day assistance
